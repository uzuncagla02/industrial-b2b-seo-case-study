# SEO Case Study — Industrial B2B Manufacturer

**Portfolio case study | Anonymized client | English version**

## Overview
This case study summarizes a 16-month SEO performance review prepared for an industrial B2B manufacturer. The original company name, brand identifiers, exact traffic volumes, commercially sensitive keyword details, competitor identities, and internal report references have been removed.

The work combined search performance analysis, keyword tracking, competitor benchmarking, technical SEO review, international SEO assessment, and an action roadmap.

> **Confidentiality note:** This is an anonymized portfolio summary. It intentionally excludes raw exports, exact monthly traffic figures, proprietary keyword lists, competitor names, and identifying company details. Indexed charts are used where a directional trend is useful; they are not raw account data.

## Scope and methodology
- Consolidated a 16-month reporting period from monthly SEO reports.
- Reviewed Google Search Console data for clicks, impressions, click-through rate, and average position.
- Reviewed Google Analytics 4 organic sessions for the earlier reporting period.
- Used third-party SEO tooling for keyword position tracking and competitor visibility comparisons.
- Reviewed AI search visibility signals, including citations and brand mentions.
- Assessed mobile performance and Core Web Vitals across homepage, category, and product-page templates.

### Measurement caveat
The source reporting changed its primary traffic measure during the period: earlier organic traffic was reported using GA4 sessions, while later reporting relied on Google Search Console clicks. These metrics are not directly interchangeable. The analysis treated the measurement change as a reporting boundary rather than interpreting it as an organic traffic spike.

## Key findings
### 1. Search visibility and rankings
- The site maintained strong positions for several high-intent industrial product queries.
- Some broad, commercially important terms remained more volatile and represented opportunities for content improvement.
- A specific product-related keyword group showed a notable ranking decline late in the review period and was prioritized for recovery.

### 2. Click-through rate opportunity
- Search impressions remained substantial, but CTR was an improvement area.
- The review recommended improving page titles and meta descriptions, aligning snippets more closely with search intent, and strengthening relevant landing pages.

### 3. Technical SEO
- Mobile performance was weak to moderate across key page templates.
- The review identified large contentful paint and interaction responsiveness as areas requiring attention.
- Recommended actions included reducing render-blocking resources, optimizing images, reducing unnecessary scripts, and improving page loading behavior.

### 4. International SEO
- The local-language experience was comparatively mature.
- English and French content coverage and freshness needed improvement.
- A German-language market entry was identified as a potential opportunity, subject to business prioritization and localization resources.

### 5. AI search visibility
- The source review indicated an increase in AI-platform citations and mentions during the later reporting period.
- Recommendations focused on expert-led content, clear question-and-answer structures, structured data, and regularly refreshed technical product content.

## Recommendations and prioritization
| Priority | Workstream | Recommended action |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | Technical SEO | Improve mobile performance and address Core Web Vitals issues |
| Critical | Content localization | Correct translation issues and refresh English/French content |
| High | Keyword recovery | Strengthen the landing page and internal links for declining product terms |
| High | Product discovery | Convert important catalogue information into crawlable product pages |
| Medium | Structured data | Add relevant schema markup to product and organization content |
| Medium | CTR | Test titles and meta descriptions against search intent |
| Medium | International SEO | Evaluate a German-language landing-page pilot |
| Ongoing | GEO / AI visibility | Publish expert content and improve factual, structured answers |
